## For managers

### Day one (about 20 minutes)
1. **Set your password.** Use the link in your welcome email. It works once and lasts 24 hours.
2. **Add your sites.** **Sites → New Site**: one for each job or plot you're running.
3. **Invite your team.** **Workers → Invite Worker**: their name and email. Each person gets an email to set their own password.
4. **Put people on sites.** **Workers → Assign to sites**. That also puts them in each site's group chat.
5. **Install it on your phone.** **Get the App** shows how. Put its QR code up in the cabin so the lads can install it too.

### This week
- **Set a few tasks** (**Tasks → New Task**). Ask for a photo when each one's done.
- **Upload the drawings** each site needs (**Drawings → Upload Drawing**).
- **Approve hours** as they come in (**Timesheets**). Query anything that's not right; the worker fixes it and resubmits. When a week is paid, **Export week** for payroll, then **Archive week**.
- **Handle material requests** as they arrive (**Materials**). Move each through Approved, Ordered, Dispatched and Delivered, and the worker is told at each step.
- **Health and safety:**
  - **Documents:** add your inductions and RAMS (**Health & Safety → Documents**) with "sign-off required" ticked.
  - **Site sign-in:** set up the sign-in poster for each site (**Sites → the site → Sign-in & QR**).
- **Make it yours** in **Branding**: your logo, app icon and colours.

### Good to know
- **Notifications:** the bell shows what's waiting for you. Allow notifications on your phone so they reach you when the app is closed.
- **Exports:** everything exports to CSV for the office or the accountant.
- **Book your onboarding call** with Dom or Matt if you haven't yet. It's free and optional.

## For workers

1. **Set your password** from the email your manager's invite sent you.
2. **Install the app.** iPhone: in Safari, tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen". Android: in Chrome, tap the three dots, then "Install app". Or scan the QR code in the cabin.
3. **Allow notifications**, so task updates and deliveries reach you.
4. **Each day:**
   - **Sign in on site:** scan the poster at the entrance. Sign out when you leave.
   - **Your jobs:** check **My Tasks**. Add a photo when a job's done and mark it Complete.
   - **Your hours:** book them in **My Hours**: daywork hours or price work, per site and plot.
   - **Materials:** need something? **Materials → New Request**, with the date you need it by. You'll see it go from Requested to Delivered.
5. **Sign the documents** you're asked to in **Health & Safety**.
6. **Report accidents and near misses** from **Health & Safety**. Only you and your managers see your reports.

Stuck? Tap **?** in the app, ask your manager, or report a problem at banksman.app/support/.
